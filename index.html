CULTIVATE NATURE

Cultivating business models to support nature.

Nature in Australia runs on goodwill, and goodwill is running out. We put real business models underneath the people and places doing the work.

What we do

We help nature ventures transition to being profitable and sustainable. We get in and share the risk and the reward.

And we help businesses put real nature programs to work: products, partnerships and projects that are good for nature and good for business.

How we work

Skin in the game

We are not consultants watching from the sidelines. We get in, carry risk alongside the venture, and win only when it does.

Donations go further

We put business models underneath the operations, so every donation and sponsorship funds the future instead of the power bill. We do not run charities and we never compete with them. We make their work go further.

Succession is our craft

When a founder needs a way forward, we transition ownership for longevity and purpose. We have spent a decade doing it for farms, and the nature sector needs it just as badly.

What we are building

  • Wildlife parks and centres finding their future, with the business plan, the partners and the visitors to match the love around them.
  • Pet food that cleans rivers: every bag sold takes a pest out of the water, with First Nations crews doing the work.
  • Virtual fencing that keeps wildlife off the roads, paid for by the people who save money when collisions never happen.
  • A program that backs the vets who treat injured wildlife for free, so doing the right thing stops costing them.
  • Stays where people meet nature, so the visit funds the place.
  • Platforms that turn love of animals into visits, learning and funding for nature, week after week.
